## **What Are All-on-4 Dental Implants?**

All-on-4 dental implants are a modern dental solution designed for patients who have multiple missing teeth or are dealing with the complications of traditional dentures. This technique uses four strategically placed implants to support a full arch of teeth, eliminating the need for bone grafting in most cases.

Unlike traditional methods that require seven or more implants, the [**All-on-4 approach maximizes**](https://medium.com/@drsmileonline247/all-on-4-implants-in-newport-beach-a-modern-approach-to-tooth-restoration-dbb680910e3e) efficiency and minimizes discomfort. The implants are biosynthetically fastened to the jawbone, offering a stable and durable foundation for prosthetic teeth. Patients often leave the dentist’s office with a fully restored smile in just one visit.

## **The Procedure: What to Expect**

One of the standout features of the All-on-4 technique is its speed and simplicity. Here’s a breakdown of what you can expect:

1.  **Consultation and Planning**: Your dentist will perform a thorough evaluation of your oral health, including X-rays and 3D imaging, to determine the best placement for the implants.
2.  **Placement of Implants**: Four titanium implants are strategically inserted into the jawbone—two at the front and two at the back, angled for maximum stability.
3.  **Immediate Loading**: A temporary dental prosthesis is attached to the implants, allowing you to leave the clinic with a functional and aesthetic set of teeth.
4.  **Healing Period**: Over the next 3-6 months, the implants integrate with your jawbone, becoming a permanent part of your oral structure.
5.  **Final Prosthesis**: Once the healing process is complete, the temporary prosthesis is replaced with a custom-designed permanent one.

The entire procedure is typically completed in a single day, making it an ideal choice for those seeking immediate results.
