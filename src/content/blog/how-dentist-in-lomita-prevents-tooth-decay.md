### Foods to avoid—major red flag!!

#### Sugary Foods and Sweets

-   Candy, chocolates, pastries, and desserts coat teeth with sugar, which bacteria convert into acid.
-   Hard candies are especially harmful because they stay in the mouth longer.

#### Sticky Snacks

-   Caramel, toffee, gummy candies, and dried fruits cling to teeth and are difficult to clean off.
-   These increase the risk of cavities spreading deeper.

#### Sugary and Carbonated Drinks

-   Soda, energy drinks, and sweetened juices are highly acidic and full of sugar.
-   They erode enamel and worsen sensitivity.

#### Acidic Foods

-   Citrus fruits (like lemons and oranges), vinegar-based foods, and pickles can weaken enamel.
-   Acidic foods make teeth more vulnerable to decay.
