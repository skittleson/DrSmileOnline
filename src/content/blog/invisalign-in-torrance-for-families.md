## FAQ

### Q. What is Invisalign, and how does it work?

Invisalign is a clear aligner system that gradually shifts your teeth using custom-made, removable trays. Each set is worn for about two weeks before switching to the next, gently guiding your teeth into alignment.

### Q. How long does Invisalign treatment take? 

Treatment typically lasts 12 to 18 months, depending on the complexity of your case and how consistently you wear the aligners (20–22 hours/day).

### Q. Is Invisalign painful? 

Most patients experience mild discomfort or pressure when switching to a new set of aligners, but it’s generally less painful than traditional braces.

### Q. Can I eat and drink with Invisalign?

 Yes! You can remove the aligners while eating or drinking. Just remember to brush your teeth before putting them back in to avoid staining or decay.

### Q. What is TMJ disorder, and what are the common symptoms?

TMJ disorder affects the temporomandibular joint, which connects your jaw to your skull. It can cause pain, clicking, jaw locking, and headaches.
