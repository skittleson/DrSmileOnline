## Types of dental services available at sedation dentistry in Newport Beach

Not all sedation is created equal. The right approach depends on the complexity of the procedure and the patient’s comfort level.

-   Nitrous oxide:

 Nitrous oxide, commonly known as laughing gas, is ideal for patients experiencing mild anxiety or undergoing short, routine dental procedures such as cleanings or simple fillings. It is administered through a small mask placed over the nose and works quickly to induce a sense of calm and mild euphoria.

-   Oral sedation:

Oral sedation involves taking a prescribed pill, typically an anti-anxiety medication like diazepam or triazolam, about an hour before the dental appointment. This method is suitable for individuals with moderate anxiety or those undergoing longer procedures.

-   IV sedation:

 IV sedation, or intravenous sedation, is used for patients with significant dental anxiety or those undergoing more complex or lengthy treatments, such as oral surgery or dental implants. The sedative is administered directly into the bloodstream, allowing for a rapid onset and precise control over the level of sedation. This method provides a deeper state of relaxation, and patients often have little to no memory of the procedure. Continuous monitoring of vital signs is necessary, and patients must be accompanied by someone who can assist them afterward, as the recovery period is longer.

-   General anesthesia:

 It is the most intensive form of sedation and is typically reserved for the most complex dental procedures or for patients with special needs, severe dental phobia, or medical conditions that make other forms of sedation unsuitable. It renders the patient completely unconscious and is usually administered in a hospital or surgical center by an anesthesiologist.
