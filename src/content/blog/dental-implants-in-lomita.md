A missing tooth doesn't just change how your smile looks — it changes how you chew, how your bite lines up, and how the surrounding teeth eventually shift. **Dental implants in Lomita** replace a missing tooth root as well as the tooth itself, so the fix is as close to natural as dentistry gets.

## What is a dental implant?

An implant is a small titanium post placed in the jawbone where a tooth root used to be. Once it bonds with the bone, a custom crown is attached on top. The result looks, feels, and functions like a natural tooth — and unlike a bridge, it doesn't rely on the teeth next to it for support.

## Single-tooth vs. full-arch implants

### Single-tooth implant

-   Replaces one missing tooth.
-   Preserves the jawbone around the gap, which otherwise shrinks over time.
-   Keeps neighboring teeth from drifting into the space.

### Full-arch (All-on-4)

When several teeth or a whole arch are missing, [**All-on-4**](/DrSmileOnline/all-on-four-prosthetics/) uses a smaller number of implants placed strategically to support a full set of fixed teeth — a practical alternative to dentures for patients who've outgrown them.

## Who is a good candidate?

Most healthy adults qualify, but a few things are checked first:

-   **Bone volume** — enough jawbone to anchor the implant, or a plan to build it up if not.
-   **Oral health** — active gum disease or infection is treated before implant work starts.
-   **Overall health** — conditions like uncontrolled diabetes or heavy smoking affect healing and are discussed openly at your consultation.

You don't need perfect teeth to qualify. You need an honest look at your bone, gums, and health — which is exactly what the consultation provides.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q. How long does a dental implant take?

A single-tooth implant is staged: placement, a healing period while it bonds to the bone (often several months), then the crown. Your consultation will give you a timeline for your specific case.

### Q. Are implants painful?

Placement is done under local anesthesia, and most patients say it's comparable to, or less than, a tooth extraction. Discomfort afterward is typically manageable with standard pain relief.

### Q. How long do implants last?

With good oral hygiene and regular checkups, implants can last decades — the titanium post is designed to be a long-term part of your jaw, and the crown can be replaced if it ever wears.

### Q. What if I don't have enough bone?

In many cases the bone can be rebuilt first with a graft. That's part of why the consultation includes imaging — to know whether you're ready now or need a preparatory step.
