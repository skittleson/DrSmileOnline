## What counts as a dental emergency in Torrance

Not every tooth problem is an emergency, but these are, and they should be seen the same day:

-   **A broken or chipped tooth** — especially if it's sharp, painful, or the nerve is exposed.
-   **A knocked-out tooth** — time-critical; the goal is to get it back in place fast.
-   **A loose or displaced tooth** after an injury.
-   **Severe, persistent pain** or a swelling that won't go down.
-   **A lost filling or crown** that has left a tooth sensitive and vulnerable.

## What to do in the first 30 minutes

### 1. Broken or chipped tooth

-   Rinse the area gently with warm water.
-   Save any broken piece — wrap it in a damp cloth and bring it in.
-   Use ice on the outside of your cheek to reduce swelling.
-   Avoid chewing on that side, and don't test the tooth by tapping it.

### 2. Knocked-out tooth (act fast)

-   Pick it up by the **crown** (the part that shows in your mouth), never the root.
-   Rinse it briefly with water — **do not scrub it** and do not peel off any attached tissue.
-   Try to place it back in the socket and bite gently on a clean cloth. If it won't stay, store it in **milk** or saliva.
-   Get to a dentist **within the hour** if you can. The faster, the better the chance of saving the tooth.

### 3. Severe pain or swelling

-   You can take over-the-counter pain relief as directed on the label.
-   Apply a cold compress to the outside of your face.
-   Avoid placing aspirin directly on the gum — it can burn the tissue.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q. How fast do I need to see a dentist for a broken tooth?

As soon as you can, ideally the same day. A broken tooth can expose the nerve and invite infection; the sooner it's evaluated and repaired, the better the chance of saving it and the less pain you'll feel.

### Q. Can a knocked-out tooth be saved?

Often, yes — but time is the deciding factor. The best chance comes from getting the tooth back in the socket (or kept in milk) and reaching a dentist within about an hour of the injury.

### Q. What if I can't get to a dentist today?

Call anyway. A Torrance dentist can often fit you in the same day or give you specific instructions to protect the tooth and manage pain until you're seen. Don't wait for the pain to "go away on its own" — that usually means the nerve is dying.
