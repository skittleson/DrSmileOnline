## Frequently Asked Questions

###      1.What causes gum disease?

Gum disease is mainly caused by plaque buildup, poor oral hygiene, smoking, hormonal changes, or certain medications. Addressing these factors can prevent their progression.

###     2.What are the symptoms of gum disease?

Common symptoms include swollen or bleeding gums, bad breath, gum recession, loose teeth, and pain while chewing. Detecting these signs early ensures prompt treatment and better outcomes.

###    3.What treatments are available for gum disease in Lomita?

Treatment options include scaling and root planning, antibiotics, laser therapy, and advanced surgeries like flap surgery and bone grafts. Your dentist will tailor treatments to your specific needs.

###    4.Can gum disease affect overall health?

Yes, untreated gum disease is linked to systemic issues like heart disease and diabetes. Proper treatment in Lomita reduces infection risks and promotes overall health.

###    5.How can I maintain healthy gums after treatment?

Brush and floss daily, use antibacterial mouthwash, visit your dentist regularly, and avoid smoking. These practices help prevent gum disease recurrence and ensure lasting oral health.
