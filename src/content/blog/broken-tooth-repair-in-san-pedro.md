## Types of Broken Teeth and What Treatment Each Requires

The right treatment depends on where the break is, how deep it goes, and whether the nerve is involved.

-   ### Chipped or cracked enamel
    

The outermost layer of the tooth is damaged, but the pulp is unaffected. This is typically treated with dental bonding or a crown, depending on the size of the chip.

-   ### Fractured cusp
    

The pointed chewing surface of the tooth breaks off. It usually does not affect the pulp and can often be restored with a crown or onlay in a single visit.

-   ### Cracked tooth
    

A crack that extends from the chewing surface downward toward the root. If it reaches the pulp, a root canal followed by a crown is needed. If it extends below the gumline, extraction may be necessary.

-   ### Split tooth
    

The tooth has fractured into two distinct segments. Depending on the position and depth of the split, part of the tooth may be saved, or a full extraction and implant may be required.

-   ### Vertical root fracture
    

A crack that starts at the root and moves upward. These are often painless until infection develops. Extraction is typically the only option.

Knowing which type you are dealing with requires a clinical exam and digital X-ray. A skilled provider offering broken tooth repair in San Pedro will assess this immediately and walk you through your options before treatment begins.

## What to Do Before You Reach the Dentist

These first-response steps can reduce pain and protect the tooth while you make your way in:

-   Rinse your mouth gently with warm salt water to clean the area.
-   If there is bleeding, apply gentle pressure with clean gauze.
-   Apply a cold compress to the outside of the cheek to reduce swelling.
-   Collect any broken pieces of the tooth and bring them with you.
-   Avoid chewing on the affected side entirely.
-   Take ibuprofen as directed for pain management.
-   If a sharp edge is cutting your tongue or cheek, cover it temporarily with dental wax or sugarless gum.

These steps manage the situation but do not treat it. Same-day broken tooth repair in San Pedro is still essential even when symptoms feel manageable.
