## FAQ’s

### **Q.** What is sedation dentistry?

 Sedation dentistry uses medication to help patients relax during dental procedures. It’s ideal for those with dental anxiety, sensitive teeth, or undergoing lengthy treatments.

### **Q.** What types of sedation are available? 

Common options include nitrous oxide (laughing gas) and IV sedation, which provide varying levels of relaxation depending on the patient’s needs.

### **Q.** Is sedation dentistry safe? 

Yes, when administered by trained professionals, sedation dentistry is safe. Dentists monitor vital signs and adjust sedation levels to ensure patient comfort and safety. 

### **Q.** What should I do before a sedation appointment?

 Before sedation dentistry, follow your dentist’s instructions—avoid food or drink for a certain period, arrange transportation, and inform your provider of any medications or health conditions to ensure a safe experience.

### **Q.** Can I drive after sedation?

 No, it’s unsafe to drive after sedation. Depending on the type used, recovery can take hours. Arrange for someone to drive you home and allow time to fully regain alertness before resuming daily activities.
