## FAQ

### Q. What is Invisalign?

 Invisalign is a modern orthodontic treatment using clear, removable aligners to straighten teeth. Unlike traditional braces, Invisalign aligners are nearly invisible and can be taken out for eating and brushing. The treatment is customized for each patient, gradually shifting teeth into the desired position.

### Q. How does Invisalign work?

 Invisalign aligners are designed using advanced 3D imaging technology. Patients receive a series of aligners, each worn for about two weeks, gently moving teeth into alignment. Regular checkups ensure progress, and the treatment duration varies based on individual needs.

### Q. How much does Invisalign cost in Torrance? 

The cost of Invisalign in Torrance depends on the complexity of the case and the number of aligners needed. Generally, it is comparable to traditional braces. Many dental clinics offer flexible payment plans, and insurance may cover part of the cost.

### Q. What are the benefits of Invisalign? 

Invisalign aligners are clear and discreet, making them a great choice for adults and teens. They are removable, allowing patients to eat freely and maintain oral hygiene easily. Unlike metal braces, Invisalign is more comfortable and does not irritate.

### Q. How long does Invisalign treatment take?

 The average Invisalign treatment lasts between 12 and 18 months, but some patients notice improvements within weeks. The duration depends on the complexity of the case and how consistently aligners are worn.
