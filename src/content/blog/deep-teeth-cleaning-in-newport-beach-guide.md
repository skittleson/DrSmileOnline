## What Is Deep Teeth Cleaning?

Deep teeth cleaning, also known as scaling and root planing, is a two-part procedure designed to remove plaque and tartar from below the gumline and smooth the surfaces of tooth roots. Unlike a routine cleaning, which focuses on the visible surfaces of teeth, deep teeth cleaning in Newport Beach targets the areas where gum disease actually begins.

The procedure typically includes:

-   Scaling to remove plaque and tartar buildup along and beneath the gumline
-   Root planing to smooth root surfaces, making it harder for bacteria to reattach
-   Localized numbing to keep the process comfortable
-   A follow-up evaluation to monitor gum healing and progress

This more thorough approach helps address the underlying causes of gum inflammation rather than just managing the surface symptoms.

## Who Needs Deep Teeth Cleaning?

Not everyone requires this level of cleaning during a routine dental visit. Deep cleaning is typically recommended for patients showing early to moderate signs of gum disease, also known as periodontal disease.

Common signs that you may benefit from deep teeth cleaning in Newport Beach include:

-   Gums that bleed easily during brushing or flossing
-   Persistent bad breath that does not improve with regular oral hygiene
-   Gum recession or teeth that appear longer than before
-   Gum pockets deeper than three millimeters, identified during a dental exam
-   Loose teeth or noticeable shifting in your bite

If your dentist identifies any of these signs during a checkup, deep cleaning is often recommended as a proactive step before gum disease progresses further.

## Key Benefits of Deep Teeth Cleaning in Newport Beach

Choosing deep teeth cleaning offers benefits that extend well beyond a fresher-feeling mouth. This procedure plays a real role in protecting long-term oral and even overall health.

### Prevents Gum Disease From Progressing

Left untreated, early gum disease can advance into more serious periodontal disease, which can lead to bone loss and eventually tooth loss. Deep teeth cleaning in Newport Beach removes the bacteria driving this progression before it reaches a more damaging stage.

### Reduces Bad Breath

Persistent bad breath is often caused by bacteria trapped beneath the gumline. Thoroughly cleaning these areas addresses the source of the odor rather than just masking it temporarily.

### Protects Against Tooth Loss

Gum disease is one of the leading causes of adult tooth loss. By removing harmful bacteria and smoothing root surfaces, deep cleaning helps gums reattach more securely to teeth, reducing the risk of loosening over time.

### Supports Overall Health

Research continues to link gum health to broader health outcomes, including cardiovascular health and diabetes management. Investing in deep teeth cleaning supports not just your smile but also your overall well-being.

### Improves the Effectiveness of Future Cleanings

Once tartar buildup below the gumline is removed, routine cleanings become more effective at maintaining oral health going forward, since your dental team is no longer working around significant buildup.
