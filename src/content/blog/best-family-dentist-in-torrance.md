### 5\. Address emergency issues

Emergencies are unpredictable and can occur at any point. So here you need to choose a Family Dentist in Torrance that can help you in emergencies.

Here is a list of issues that fall under emergency dental problems

#### 1.Severe Toothache

-   Often caused by decay, infection, or trauma.
-   Signals an underlying problem that needs urgent attention.
-   Pain can spread to the jaw or ear if untreated.

#### 2\. Knocked-Out Tooth

-   Happens due to accidents or sports injuries.
-   Immediate action is critical: pick up the tooth by the crown, rinse gently, and try to place it back in the socket or store it in milk until reaching a dentist.

#### 3\. Broken or Chipped Tooth

-   Can result from biting hard foods or accidents.
-   May expose nerves, causing pain and sensitivity.
-   Needs quick repair to prevent infection.

#### 4\. Dental Abscess or Infection

-   A pocket of pus caused by bacterial infection.
-   Symptoms include swelling, fever, and severe pain.
-   Can spread to other parts of the body if untreated.

#### 5\. Lost or Damaged Filling/Crown

-   Exposes sensitive tooth surfaces.
-   Can cause pain and increase the risk of further decay.
-   Requires urgent replacement to restore protection.

#### 6\. Soft Tissue Injuries

-   Cuts or wounds to gums, lips, or tongue.
-   May involve bleeding and swelling.
-   Needs immediate care to prevent complications
