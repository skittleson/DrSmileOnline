## FAQ

### Q. What is sedation dentistry, and how does it help with dental anxiety?

Sedation dentistry uses medication to help patients relax during dental procedures. It’s especially helpful for those with dental phobia, past traumatic experiences, or a strong gag reflex. By easing anxiety, patients are more likely to receive timely care and avoid worsening oral health issues.

### Q. Will I be unconscious during sedation?

Not necessarily. Most sedation methods—like oral sedatives or nitrous oxide (laughing gas)—induce a deep state of relaxation while keeping you conscious and responsive. You may not remember much of the procedure afterward, which many patients find comforting.

### Q. What types of dental procedures can be done under sedation?

Almost any procedure can be performed with sedation, from routine cleanings to complex smile makeovers, root canals, tooth extractions, and dental implants. Sedation allows multiple treatments to be completed in one visit, making it ideal for patients with extensive dental needs.

### Q.  Can I drive myself home after sedation?

If you receive oral or IV sedation, you’ll need someone to drive you home and stay with you until the effects wear off. However, with nitrous oxide, the effects fade quickly, and you may be able to resume normal activities shortly after your appointment.

###  Q. Is sedation dentistry covered by insurance?

Coverage varies. Many insurance plans do not cover sedation unless it’s deemed medically necessary. However, some Newport Beach dental offices offer financing options or payment plans to make sedation more accessible.
