## Frequently Asked Questions :

### Q. How many visits are typically required for dental veneers in Lomita?

Most patients typically require two to three visits. The first is for the assessment and preparation of the teeth, while the final visit is dedicated to the precise placement and final bonding of your dental veneers.

### Q. Does the procedure for dental veneers in Lomita hurt?

No. We prioritize your comfort above all else by using local anesthesia during the tooth preparation phase. Most patients report feeling little to no discomfort, finding the process of getting dental veneers in Lomita to be a very straightforward and pleasant experience.

### Q. Can I get dental veneers in Lomita if I have gum disease?

We must treat any active gum disease, decay, or infection before proceeding with cosmetic work. A healthy biological foundation is required for the long-term success and aesthetic longevity of dental veneers in Lomita.

### Q. What is the most important aspect of teeth cleaning after veneers?

The most crucial aspect is ensuring your gum line remains healthy and free of debris. Consistent brushing twice daily, daily flossing, and visiting our office for professional hygiene appointments will prevent plaque buildup that could eventually compromise the longevity of your veneers.

### Q. Does a tooth extraction in Lomita always happen before veneers?

Absolutely not. A tooth extraction in Lomita is strictly a restorative measure performed only if a specific tooth is too damaged, infected, or decayed to be saved.
