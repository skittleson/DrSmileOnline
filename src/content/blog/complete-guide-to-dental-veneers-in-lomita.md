## The Dental Veneers Process at Dr. Smile: Step by Step

Understanding what the process looks like helps patients feel prepared and confident before they begin.

### Step 1: Consultation and Assessment

Your dentist examines your teeth and discusses your smile goals. Digital X-rays and imaging are taken to assess the health of your teeth and gums. Veneers are only placed on teeth that are structurally sound and free from active decay or gum disease.

### Step 2: Tooth Preparation

A small amount of enamel is gently removed from the front surface of each tooth receiving a veneer. This creates space for the veneer shell and ensures a natural fit. Local anesthesia keeps this step comfortable.

### Step 3: Impressions and Temporary Veneers

Precise impressions are taken and sent to a dental laboratory where your custom veneers are fabricated. Temporary veneers are placed in the meantime to protect your teeth and give you a preview of your new smile.

### Step 4: Permanent Bonding

Once your veneers are ready, the temporaries are removed, and the permanent veneers are bonded to your teeth using a strong dental adhesive. The fit, shape, and color are checked carefully before the bond is finalized. Adjustments are made on the spot if needed.

### Step 5: Follow-Up

A follow-up visit confirms that your bite feels natural and that the veneers are performing as expected. Your dentist provides guidance on care and maintenance to maximize the lifespan of your veneers.

Dental veneers in Lomita at Dr. Smile typically require two to three visits from start to finish. The result is a completely transformed smile that can last ten to fifteen years with proper care.

## Frequently Asked Questions :

### Q. How long do dental veneers in Lomita last?

With proper care and regular dental checkups, porcelain veneers typically last between ten and fifteen years. Composite veneers have a shorter lifespan of five to seven years, but can be repaired more easily if chipped or damaged.

### Q. Are dental veneers painful to get?

The tooth preparation step involves removing a small amount of enamel, which is done under local anesthesia. Most patients report little to no discomfort during the procedure. Some mild sensitivity after preparation is normal and resolves quickly.

### Q. Can veneers be placed over unhealthy teeth?

No. Veneers require a healthy foundation. Any decay, gum disease, or structural damage must be treated before veneer placement. Your dentist will complete a full assessment at the consultation to confirm your teeth are ready.

### Q. Do veneers require special care after placement?

Veneers do not require special products, but good oral hygiene is essential. Brush twice daily, floss regularly, and attend checkups every six months. Avoiding biting hard objects and wearing a night guard if you grind your teeth will protect your veneers and extend their lifespan.

### Q. How do I know if I need veneers or another cosmetic treatment?

Your dentist will review your smile goals and assess your teeth during the consultation. Veneers are recommended when multiple cosmetic concerns need to be addressed together. For single issues like staining alone, whitening may be sufficient. Your dentist will always recommend the most conservative option that achieves your goals.
