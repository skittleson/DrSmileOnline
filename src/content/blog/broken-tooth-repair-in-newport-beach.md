Once you arrive, your dentist will assess the damage and recommend the most appropriate treatment. Here’s what Broken Tooth Repair in Newport Beach typically looks like, depending on the severity:

For small chips that haven’t reached the nerve, dental bonding is usually the fastest and most affordable fix. A tooth-colored resin is applied, shaped, and hardened — often in a single visit.

If a larger piece has broken off, a dental crown may be recommended. Crowns cover the entire visible portion of the tooth, restoring its shape, strength, and appearance.

## How to Prevent a Broken Tooth in the Future

Once your tooth is repaired, a few simple habits can go a long way in protecting it:

-   Wear a mouthguard during contact sports or any physical activity
-   Avoid chewing on hard objects like ice, hard candy, or pen caps
-   Don’t use your teeth as tools to open bottles or packaging
-   Visit your dentist regularly so small cracks are caught before they become breaks
