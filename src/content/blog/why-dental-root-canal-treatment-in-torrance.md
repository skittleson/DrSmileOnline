## Frequently asked questions

### What is a Dental Root Canal Treatment?

A root canal treatment is a dental procedure designed to save a tooth that has been severely damaged or infected. It involves removing the infected pulp inside the tooth, cleaning and disinfecting the area, and sealing it to prevent further infection.

### Why is Root Canal Treatment Essential for Tooth Preservation?

Root canal treatment helps preserve your natural tooth by eliminating infection and preventing the need for extraction. Keeping your natural teeth maintains proper chewing function, jawbone structure, and overall oral health.

### What are the Signs That I Might Need a Root Canal?

Common symptoms include persistent tooth pain, sensitivity to hot or cold, swelling around the tooth, discoloration, or the presence of pimples on the gums. If you experience any of these, consult a dentist in Torrance promptly.

### Is Root Canal Treatment Painful?

Modern root canal treatments are performed under local anesthesia, making the procedure virtually painless. Any discomfort after the treatment is usually mild and temporary, often manageable with over-the-counter pain relievers.

### How Can I Prevent the Need for a Root Canal?

Maintaining good oral hygiene, such as brushing and flossing regularly, and visiting your dentist for routine checkups, can help prevent tooth decay and infections that might lead to the need for a root canal.
