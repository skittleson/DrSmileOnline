## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q. How does gum disease affect the immune system?

Gum disease causes chronic inflammation in the mouth, which can trigger systemic inflammatory responses throughout the body. This persistent immune activation can weaken the body’s ability to respond to other infections and illnesses, making it harder to maintain overall health.

### Q. Can treating gum disease really improve my overall health?

Yes, treating gum disease can positively impact overall health. Periodontal therapy reduces harmful bacteria and inflammation, which are linked to conditions such as heart disease, diabetes, and respiratory issues.

### Q. What are the signs that I might need professional gum disease treatment in Lomita?

Common signs include bleeding gums, persistent bad breath, gum recession, loose teeth, and swelling or tenderness in the gums. If you experience any of these symptoms, it’s important to seek a professional evaluation.

### Q. Is professional treatment necessary if I brush and floss regularly?

While daily brushing and flossing are essential, they may not be enough to remove plaque and tartar buildup below the gumline. Professional treatment is necessary to thoroughly clean these areas and address any underlying infection.

### Q. How often should I follow up after this treatment?

Follow-up frequency depends on the severity of your condition and your dentist’s recommendations. Typically, patients are advised to return every three to six months for maintenance cleanings and evaluations.
