## FAQ

### Q. What is cosmetic dentistry?

 Cosmetic dentistry focuses on improving the appearance of teeth, gums, and smiles. It includes treatments like teeth whitening, veneers, bonding, and crowns. These procedures enhance aesthetics while maintaining functionality, helping patients achieve a confident, radiant smile.

### Q. What are the benefits of cosmetic dentistry?

Cosmetic dentistry improves self-confidence, enhances oral health, and corrects imperfections like discoloration, gaps, and misalignment. It also strengthens teeth, restores damaged enamel, and provides long-lasting results, ensuring a beautiful and functional smile.

### Q. How long do cosmetic dental treatments last? 

The longevity of cosmetic treatments depends on the procedure and maintenance. Teeth whitening lasts months, veneers and crowns can last over a decade, and implants are permanent with proper care. Regular dental visits and good oral hygiene extend their lifespan.

### Q. Is cosmetic dentistry painful?

Most cosmetic procedures are minimally invasive and performed with anesthesia or sedation for comfort. Patients may experience mild sensitivity or discomfort, but recovery is quick. Dentists ensure a painless experience with advanced techniques and personalized care.

### Q. What is the difference between cosmetic and general dentistry?

 A cosmetic dentist in Newport Beach focuses on enhancing the appearance of teeth, while a general dentist prioritizes oral health and function. Cosmetic treatments include veneers, teeth whitening, and bonding, whereas general dentistry covers cleanings, fillings, and preventive care. Many cosmetic procedures also improve dental health, blending aesthetics with functionality.
