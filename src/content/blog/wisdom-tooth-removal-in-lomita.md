## What Are Wisdom Teeth and Why Do They Cause Problems?

Wisdom teeth are the last set of molars to grow. They usually appear between the ages of 17 and 25. Most people do not have enough space in their jaws to accommodate them. This causes several issues, including:

-   Impaction, where the tooth gets stuck beneath the gum
-   Crowding, which pushes nearby teeth out of alignment
-   Infection, especially when the tooth partially erupts
-   Cysts, which can damage the surrounding bone and teeth
-   Pain and swelling that make eating and speaking difficult

A local general dentist can examine your wisdom teeth and tell you whether removal is necessary. Not every wisdom tooth needs to come out, but many do.
