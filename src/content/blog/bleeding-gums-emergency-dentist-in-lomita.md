## What Bleeding Gums Are Actually Telling You

Your gums don’t bleed without a reason. There’s always something going on underneath.

The most common causes include:

-   Plaque and tartar buildup along the gum line
-   Early or advanced gum disease
-   Aggressive brushing or flossing
-   A mouth injury or trauma
-   Vitamin deficiencies, especially Vitamin C and K
-   Certain medications that thin the blood

Minor bleeding after flossing is sometimes normal. But if it keeps happening — or if your gums are swollen, red, or tender — that’s a different story. That’s when bleeding gums treatment becomes urgent.

## Is It a Dental Emergency?

Not every bleeding gum situation is a full emergency. But some definitely are. You need to see an emergency dentist in Lomita immediately if

-   Bleeding doesn’t stop after 10 to 15 minutes
-   Your gums are severely swollen and painful
-   You notice pus or a bad taste in your mouth
-   A tooth feels loose alongside the bleeding
-   The bleeding started after a fall or a mouth injury

These are signs that something serious is happening. A swollen gums emergency should never be brushed off or managed with home remedies alone.

## What to Do Right Now — Before You Reach the Dentist

While you arrange your appointment, here are the steps to manage the bleeding at home.

-   Rinse with warm salt water: Mix half a teaspoon of salt in a glass of warm water. Swish gently for 30 seconds. This reduces bacteria and soothes irritated tissue.
-   Apply gentle pressure: Use clean gauze or a damp cloth. Press it softly against the bleeding area for a few minutes.
-   Use a cold compress: If your gums are swollen, hold an ice pack against your cheek. Do this for 10 minutes at a time. It slows bleeding and reduces inflammation.
-   Brush gently: Don’t stop brushing. Just switch to a soft-bristled toothbrush. Use slow, circular motions. Avoid scrubbing hard against the gum line.
-   Skip the irritants: No smoking. No alcohol. No spicy or acidic foods. These slow down healing and make bleeding worse.

These steps help temporarily. They are not a cure. You still need proper bleeding gums treatment from a professional.

## Why Gum Disease Is Usually the Culprit

Here’s the truth most people don’t want to hear. Most cases of bleeding gums are linked to gum disease. Gum disease starts silently. There’s usually no pain in the early stages. But the bacteria are already attacking your gum tissue and the bone beneath it.

-   Stage one is gingivitis. This is reversible with proper cleaning and care.
-   Stage two is periodontitis. This is more serious and requires professional intervention.

If you’re dealing with repeat bleeding, don’t assume it’s just from brushing too hard. Get evaluated. Gum disease treatment can stop the damage before it becomes permanent.

### Q. When should I call an emergency dentist in Lomita for bleeding gums?

Call immediately if bleeding doesn’t stop within 15 minutes, if your gums are severely swollen, or if you see signs of infection like pus or a foul taste.

### Q.Can bleeding gums go away on their own?

Minor bleeding from irritation might improve with better brushing habits. But persistent bleeding almost always needs professional bleeding gums treatment to resolve fully.

### Q.What is the difference between gingivitis and periodontitis?

Gingivitis is early-stage gum disease and is reversible. Periodontitis is more advanced, affecting bone and tissue. Both require gum disease treatment from a qualified dentist.

### Q. Is a swollen gum an emergency, life-threatening?

In rare cases, yes. If the infection spreads to your jaw or neck, it can become dangerous quickly. Never ignore a swollen gum— get evaluated immediately.
