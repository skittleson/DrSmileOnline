## What Is Gum Disease and Why Does It Happen?

Gum disease starts quietly. It begins with plaque — a sticky film of bacteria — building up along your gumline. When plaque isn’t removed through regular brushing and flossing, it hardens into tartar. Tartar irritates the gum tissue and causes inflammation.

Common causes of gum disease include:

-   Poor brushing and flossing habits
-   Smoking or tobacco use
-   Dry mouth from medications or health conditions
-   Hormonal changes during pregnancy or menopause
-   Diabetes and other systemic health issues
-   Genetics — some people are simply more prone to gum problems

No matter the cause, a deep cleaning dentist in Newport Beach can assess your gum health and create a treatment plan that fits your specific needs.

## Signs You May Need to See a Deep Cleaning Dentist in Newport Beach

Here are the warning signs that mean it’s time to book an appointment:

-   Bleeding gums: Healthy gums don’t bleed when you brush or floss. Bleeding is a sign of inflammation.
-   Swollen gums: Puffy or tender gums that don’t resolve on their own need professional attention. Swollen gums treatment should not be delayed — it gets harder to manage the longer it’s ignored.
-   Receding gums: When gums pull back from the teeth, it exposes the root and creates sensitivity. This is a sign of advancing gum disease and warrants prompt receding gums treatment.
-   Persistent bad breath: Bacteria below the gumline produce odor that brushing alone can’t fix.
-   Loose teeth: Bone loss from periodontitis can make teeth feel unstable.
-   Pain when chewing: Discomfort during meals can indicate infected gum tissue or bone damage.

## Frequently Asked Questions :

### Q. What does a deep cleaning dentist in Newport Beach actually do differently from a regular cleaning?

A regular cleaning removes plaque and tartar above the gumline. A deep cleaning dentist in Newport Beach goes below the gumline using scaling and root planing to remove infection from the pockets between your teeth and gums — something a standard cleaning simply cannot address.

### Q. How do I know if I need scaling and root planing in Newport Beach?

If your gum pockets measure 4mm or deeper during a periodontal exam, you likely need scaling and root planing.  Other signs include bleeding, persistent bad breath, and visible tartar buildup below the gumline.

### Q. Is receding gums treatment painful?
