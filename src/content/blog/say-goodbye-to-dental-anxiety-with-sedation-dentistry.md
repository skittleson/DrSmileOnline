## What Is Sedation Dentistry?

Sedation dentistry involves administering medication to help patients feel calm and relaxed during dental treatments. This approach is ideal for individuals who experience fear, discomfort, or nervousness at the dentist’s office.

At Doctor Smile, sedation isn’t just about easing anxiety—it’s also about creating an environment where dental care becomes accessible, painless, and even enjoyable. ## Sedation Options for Every Level of Anxiety

Doctor Smile offers multiple sedation options to address varying degrees of dental anxiety and treatment needs:

## Oral Sedation

Oral sedation is ideal for mild to moderate dental anxiety. Patients take a prescribed sedative, such as Halcion, about an hour before their appointment. This medication induces a relaxed state while keeping the patient awake and responsive. Oral sedation is perfect for treatments such as periodontal therapy, crowns, fillings, and procedures requiring an oral surgeon.

## IV Sedation

IV sedation offers a deeper level of relaxation and is suited for patients with significant dental anxiety or those undergoing complex treatments. Administered by a board-certified anesthesiologist, this sedation method keeps you calm and unaware of the procedure while remaining responsive to instructions. IV sedation is often recommended for extractions, implant surgeries, or full-mouth reconstructions, including those involving an oral surgeon.

## Nitrous Oxide

Commonly referred to as “laughing gas,” nitrous oxide is an effective option for managing mild to moderate anxiety. This odorless, colorless gas is inhaled during the procedure, creating a calming, euphoric feeling. Nitrous oxide is frequently used for routine cleanings, fillings, and other less invasive treatments, making it a versatile choice for patients of all ages.
