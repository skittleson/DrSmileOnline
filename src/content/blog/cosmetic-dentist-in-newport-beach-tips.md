## FAQ—Frequently Asked Questions 

### Q. What are the key differences between Invisalign and braces?

Invisalign uses clear, removable aligners, while braces consist of metal or ceramic brackets with wires. Invisalign offers discreet treatment and flexibility, whereas braces provide precise control for complex cases. Dr Smile’s  Cosmetic Dentist in Newport Beach can help determine the best option.

###  Q. Which treatment is faster: Invisalign or braces?

Treatment duration depends on individual cases. Invisalign typically takes 12–18 months, while braces may require 18–24 months for complex corrections. A Cosmetic Dentist in Newport Beach will assess your needs and recommend the most efficient solution for your smile.

###  Q. Is Invisalign more comfortable than braces?

Yes, Invisalign aligners are smooth and removable, reducing irritation and dietary restrictions. Braces, however, may cause discomfort due to brackets and wires. A Cosmetic Dentist in Newport Beach can guide you on managing discomfort and maintaining oral hygiene during treatment.

### Q. Can Invisalign treat all orthodontic issues?

Invisalign is effective for mild to moderate misalignment, gaps, and bite issues. However, braces are better suited for severe cases. Consulting a Cosmetic Dentist in Newport Beach ensures you receive the most suitable treatment for your dental condition.

### Q. How do I maintain my Invisalign aligners or braces?

Invisalign requires regular cleaning and proper storage when removed. Braces need careful brushing and flossing to prevent plaque buildup. A Cosmetic Dentist in Newport Beach provides personalized care instructions to ensure optimal results and long-term oral health.
