**What Is Sedation Dentistry?**

Sedation dentistry involves using medication to help patients relax during dental procedures. It’s an effective way to manage anxiety, making dental care accessible and pain-free. Depending on the patient’s needs and level of apprehension, different sedation methods are available.

**Sedation Options Designed for Comfort**

Doctor Smile provides several sedation methods tailored to each patient’s unique needs and comfort level:

**Oral Sedation**

Oral sedation is an excellent option for individuals with mild to moderate anxiety. Administered in the form of a prescription pill, usually Halcion, oral sedation is taken about an hour before your appointment. While it helps you feel calm and relaxed, you remain awake and responsive throughout the procedure. Treatments like periodontal therapy, fillings, crowns, and All on 4 dental implants can all be completed under oral sedation.

**IV Sedation**

For patients with moderate to severe dental anxiety or those undergoing complex procedures, IV sedation offers a deeper level of relaxation. Administered by a board-certified anesthesiologist, this method ensures that you remain calm and unaware of the procedure while still responsive to instructions. IV sedation is often used for extractions, dental implant surgeries, or full-mouth reconstructions, making it ideal for patients requiring All on 4 dental implants.

**Nitrous Oxide**

Commonly known as “laughing gas,” nitrous oxide is a safe and effective method to manage mild to moderate anxiety during dental treatments. This odorless, colorless gas is inhaled before and during the procedure, producing a calming, euphoric sensation. Nitrous oxide is frequently used for routine cleanings, extractions, and other minor procedures and is suitable for patients of all ages.
