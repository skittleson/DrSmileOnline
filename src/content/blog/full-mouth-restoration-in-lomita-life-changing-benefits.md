## Dental efficacies during a full Mouth Restoration in Lomita—types of restoration one can achieve.

In this section of the article, we shall explore some of the most common phases that are involved in a full Mouth Restoration in Lomita.

### 1\. Dental fillings

-   These are used to repair the teeth that have been damaged by decay or minor fractures. 
-   Traditional  fillings are typically made of amalgam or composite resin
-   Dental fillings are placed directly into the cavity after removing the decayed portion of the tooth. 

### 2\. Dental crowns

-   Known as caps, these are often custom-made; the common terminology for these is prosthetic restorations. 
-   They cover the entire visible surface of the tooth.
-   Crowns are used to strengthen and protect weakened or damaged teeth. 
-   They are available in different shapes and sizes and are known for improving the appearance of the teeth. 

### 3\. Dental bridges

-   These are used to replace one or more missing teeth by anchoring artificial teeth—the process is called pontics. 
-   Laid adjacent to the natural teeth or dental implants 
-   They help in restoring proper chewing function.
-   Maintains the facial structure and prevents the remaining teeth from shifting out of position. 

### 4\. Dental implants

These are titanium posts that are surgically placed into the jawbone to serve as the artificial tooth roots. 

Once these are integrated with the surrounding bone, the implants have the propensity to support crowns, bridges, or dentures, providing a durable and long-lasting solution for replacing missing teeth.
