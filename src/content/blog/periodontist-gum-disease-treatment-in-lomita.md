In this section of the article, we shall explore the methods used by a periodontist in Gum Disease Treatment in Lomita. This section will be two-fold, exploring both normal and advanced treatments. 

### Normal gum treatments :

#### 1.Saving and root planning

 A deep cleaning procedure that involves removing plaque and tartar from both the tooth and root surfaces to cleanse gum pockets. Ideal for deep cleaning

#### 2.Antibiotic therapy

This  Gum Disease Treatment in Lomita is known for direct delivery of antibiotics (like Atridox, PerioChip, and Arestin) or medicated mouthwashes that can be used after scaling and root planing to reduce bacterial infection.

#### 3.Gingivitis

Ideal for early-stage gum disease (gingivitis), professional cleaning combined with consistent home care is often sufficient to reverse the condition. 

### Surgical and advanced treatments :

#### 1.Flap Surgery (Pocket Reduction Surgery)

Designed for advanced periodontitis, this procedure forms a flap in the gum tissue to facilitate deep cleaning and elimination of infected tissue from extensive pockets. This is generally done by a professional by performing an  **[Oral Surgery in Lomita](/DrSmileOnline/oral-surgery/)**. 

#### 2.Bone Grafting:

 When there is bone loss, a periodontist can inject graft material into the impacted regions to rebuild the supportive bone framework.

#### 3.Soft Tissue Grafting:

To correct gum recession, a periodontist may use tissue from a different region in the mouth or from a donor to implant onto the receded gums.

#### 4.Guided Tissue Regeneration (GTR)

A specific surgical method that employs a barrier membrane to stop gum tissue from encroaching on the area where bone regeneration is occurring.

#### 5.Laser Treatment:

 A low-impact option that employs lasers to accurately eliminate infected tissue and bacteria, frequently resulting in reduced pain and a faster healing process.

## Frequently asked questions :

### Q: What does a periodontist do in treating gum disease?

A periodontist specializes in diagnosing, preventing, and treating gum disease, including gingivitis and periodontitis. In Lomita, periodontists perform procedures like scaling and root planing (deep cleaning), apply localized antibiotics like ARESTIN, and may use non-invasive methods such as Perio Protect to eliminate bacteria and promote gum healing.

### Q: How is gum disease diagnosed by a periodontist?

Diagnosis typically involves a clinical examination of the gums, measuring pocket depths around teeth, and checking for bleeding, gum recession, and tooth mobility. Advanced imaging may be used to assess bone loss. Periodontists in Lomita use these diagnostics to create personalized treatment plans.

### Q: What treatments are available for advanced gum disease in Lomita?

For advanced cases (periodontitis), Lomita periodontists offer:

-   ARESTIN Therapy: Antibiotic placed in gum pockets.
-   Perio Protect Trays: Custom trays that deliver medication to infected areas. These treatments aim to reduce infection, promote gum reattachment, and prevent tooth loss.
