## What is Invisalign?

Invisalign replaces the traditional wires and brackets of braces with a sequence of custom, clear aligners. Each set is worn for about two weeks and nudges your teeth a little further toward the final result. Because the aligners come out, brushing and flossing stay easy — no working around brackets.

## Who is Invisalign in Lomita right for?

-   **Teens** who want straighter teeth without the look of braces at school.
-   **Adults** correcting crowding, gaps, or bite issues that were never treated growing up.
-   **Anyone** who wants a straighter smile without a visible sign they're in treatment.

Invisalign handles a wide range of cases, from mild crowding to more involved alignment. Whether your case is a fit is something a real exam answers — not a guess.

## How treatment works, step by step

1.  **Examination and assessment.** A full look at your teeth, jaw, and bite, with digital imaging of the structure underneath.
2.  **A personalized treatment plan.** Your teeth's shape and position determine how the plan is mapped out — this isn't a one-size-fits-all process.
3.  **Digital scanning and mapping.** A digital scan charts exactly how your teeth need to move, set by set.
4.  **Aligners made for you.** Each set applies gentle, steady pressure. You wear them 20–22 hours a day and move to the next set as your teeth shift.
5.  **Regular checkups.** Periodic visits track progress and let the team fine-tune the plan if needed.

## Caring for your aligners

-   Remove them to eat and drink — anything besides water.
-   Brush your teeth before putting them back in, to avoid trapping food or bacteria against your teeth.
-   Clean the aligners themselves gently; avoid hot water, which can warp the plastic.
-   Keep the case with you — a lost aligner slows down your whole plan.
-   Wear them for the full 20–22 hours a day; skipping hours is the most common reason treatment runs long.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q. How long does Invisalign treatment take?

Most cases run somewhere between 6 and 18 months, depending on how much movement is needed and how consistently the aligners are worn. Your consultation will give you a specific estimate for your case.

### Q. Is Invisalign painful?

Most patients feel mild pressure for a day or two after switching to a new set of aligners — generally milder than the discomfort associated with traditional braces.

### Q. Can teens use Invisalign?

Yes, many teens are good candidates, especially for crowding or misalignment. An exam is the only way to know for sure, and treating it while the jaw is still developing can make the process smoother.

### Q. What happens if I lose an aligner?

Call the office right away. Depending on where you are in treatment, you may be able to move to the next set early or need a replacement made — either way, don't skip wearing an aligner altogether.
