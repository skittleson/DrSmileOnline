## Frequently asked questions:

### Q. What does a family dentist do?

A family dentist provides dental care for patients of all ages, from children to seniors. They handle routine checkups, cleanings, preventive care, and more advanced treatments, all in one office.

### Q. How often should my family visit the dentist?

Most families benefit from visiting the dentist every six months. Regular checkups help prevent cavities, gum disease, and other dental problems before they become serious.

### Q. Can children and adults see the same dentist?

Yes. A family dentist is trained to treat both children and adults. This makes it easier for families to schedule appointments together and build trust with one dental team.

### Q. What services are usually offered?

Family dentists provide a wide range of services, including cleanings, fillings, orthodontics, cosmetic treatments like whitening or veneers, dental implants, and emergency care.

### Q. Why is preventive care important?

Preventive care helps stop problems before they start. Regular exams, cleanings, fluoride treatments, and sealants protect teeth and save families from costly treatments later.
