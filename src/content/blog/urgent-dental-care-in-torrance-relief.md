## What Counts as a Dental Emergency?

Not every toothache is an emergency, but some situations require immediate attention. Here are the most common ones:

-   Severe or sudden tooth pain that doesn’t go away
-   A cracked or broken tooth caused by an accident or hard food
-   A knocked-out tooth that needs re-implantation within minutes
-   Swelling in the jaw or gums could indicate an infection
-   A lost crown or filling that leaves the tooth exposed and sensitive
-   Bleeding in the mouth that does not stop on its own

## What to Do Before You Arrive at Our Office

While on your way to see us for urgent dental care in Torrance, there are a few steps that can help manage the situation:

-   For a knocked-out tooth: Keep it moist by placing it in milk or holding it between your cheek and gum
-   For a cracked tooth: Rinse gently with warm water and avoid chewing on that side
-   For swelling: Apply a cold pack on the outside of the cheek for short intervals
-   For bleeding: Apply gentle pressure with a clean cloth or gauze
-   For pain: Take an over-the-counter pain reliever as directed on the label

These steps will not replace treatment, but they can help reduce discomfort until you arrive.
