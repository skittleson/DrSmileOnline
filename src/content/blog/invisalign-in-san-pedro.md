You don't have to choose between straightening your teeth and looking like you're straightening your teeth. **Invisalign in San Pedro** uses clear, removable aligners instead of metal brackets, so the treatment stays private while your smile improves. Here's what it involves.

## What is Invisalign?

Invisalign is a clear-aligner system: a series of custom, transparent trays that gradually shift your teeth into position. Each set is worn for about two weeks before you switch to the next, and because the aligners are removable, eating and cleaning your teeth stay simple.

## Why patients choose the discreet route

-   **Nearly invisible** — most people won't notice you're wearing them.
-   **Removable** — no food restrictions, no special brushing routine around brackets.
-   **Predictable** — the digital plan shows roughly where your teeth are headed before treatment even starts.
-   **Comfortable** — no wires to catch on your cheeks or lips.

This is exactly why Invisalign has become the go-to option for adults who didn't get braces as kids, and for teens who don't want the look of traditional orthodontics.

## How treatment works, step by step

1.  **Examination and assessment.** Your teeth, jaw, and bite are evaluated, with digital imaging to see the bone and root structure.
2.  **A personalized treatment plan.** The plan is built around your specific case — not a generic template.
3.  **Digital scanning and mapping.** A digital scan of your teeth becomes the roadmap for each set of aligners.
4.  **Aligners made for you.** You wear each set 20–22 hours a day, moving to the next as your teeth respond.
5.  **Regular checkups.** Short visits keep treatment on schedule and catch anything that needs adjusting.

## Who is a good candidate?

Invisalign handles a broad range of cases — crowding, gaps, and many bite issues. It's not automatically right for every case (some more complex bite problems still call for traditional orthodontics), which is why the first real answer comes from an exam, not a guess based on how your teeth look in the mirror.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q. How long does Invisalign take in San Pedro?

Treatment length depends on your case, but many patients complete treatment within a year to a year and a half. Your consultation gives you a specific estimate based on your teeth.

### Q. Can I eat and drink normally with Invisalign?

Yes. You remove the aligners to eat and drink anything besides water, then brush before putting them back in.

### Q. Is Invisalign as effective as braces?

For most crowding, spacing, and bite cases, yes — Invisalign achieves comparable results. More complex cases are sometimes better suited to traditional braces, which your exam will clarify.

### Q. What if I'm not consistent about wearing my aligners?

Treatment slows down and can shift out of plan. The 20–22 hour daily target isn't arbitrary — it's what keeps the timeline on track.
