## Frequently asked questions

### Q. What procedures do oral surgeons typically perform?

 Oral surgeons handle a wide range of procedures, including wisdom tooth extractions, dental implants, corrective jaw surgery, treatment of facial trauma, and biopsies for oral pathology.

### Q. How do I know if I need an oral surgeon instead of a general dentist?

 A general dentist manages routine care, but if your case involves complex extractions, jaw alignment issues, or surgical implant placement, you’ll likely be referred to an oral surgeon for specialized treatment.

### Q. What should I look for when choosing an oral surgeon in Torrance?

Key factors include board certification, years of experience, patient reviews, use of modern technology, and a compassionate approach to care.

### Q. Is oral surgery painful?

Most procedures are performed under local anesthesia, sedation, or general anesthesia, so patients typically feel little to no pain during surgery. Some discomfort afterward is normal, but it can be managed with prescribed medication and proper aftercare.

### Q. How long does recovery take after oral surgery?

 Recovery varies depending on the procedure. Simple extractions may heal within a few days, while more complex surgeries like jaw correction can take several weeks. Following your surgeon’s aftercare instructions is essential for a smooth recovery.

### Q. What role do stem cells play in oral surgery?

Stem cells, particularly mesenchymal stem cells (MSCs), can regenerate bone, periodontal ligaments, and soft tissues. They are being studied for applications in dental implants, jaw reconstruction, and healing after extractions.
