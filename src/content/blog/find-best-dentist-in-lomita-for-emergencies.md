## Frequently Asked Questions :

### 1\. Why is regular dental checkup important?

Regular dental checkups help prevent cavities, gum disease, and other oral health problems. They allow dentists to catch issues early before they become serious, saving both time and money in the long run.

### 2\. How often should I visit the dentist?

Most people should visit the dentist every six months for a cleaning and exam. However, those with ongoing dental issues may need more frequent visits as advised by their dentist.

### 3\. What should I do if I have a toothache?

If you have a toothache, rinse your mouth with warm water and gently floss to remove any debris. If the pain continues, schedule an appointment with a dentist as soon as possible to identify the cause.

### 4\. Are dental X-rays safe?

Yes, dental X-rays are safe. Modern digital X-rays use very low levels of radiation, and dentists take precautions to minimize exposure while ensuring accurate results.

### 5\. How can I keep my teeth healthy at home?

Brush twice a day with fluoride toothpaste, floss daily, and limit sugary foods and drinks. Drinking plenty of water and eating a balanced diet also support strong teeth and gums.
