Once the professionals have decided and identified the correct spot, it is now time for the placement of dental implants in San Pedro**.** At Dr. Smile, we comply with this procedure using local anesthesia. A small titanium post is surgically placed into the jawbone, replacing the missing tooth.

### 3\. Healing process

After the placement is successfully done, Osseointegration begins. Here, the implants fuse with the jawbone, and now this depends on the person’s healing propensity. The overall healing time of dental implantation is 3-5 months, but the initial recovery is somewhere between 1 and 3 weeks. 

### 4\. Abutment placement

This step occurs after the healing process and is similar to that of the groundwork for the final transformation.  Once the implant has been successfully fused with the jawbone during the healing phase, the abutment is inserted; consider this the connector piece that bridges the gap between the implant and the artificial tooth.

## Frequently asked questions

### 1\. How long do dental implants last? 

With proper care and maintenance, dental implants in San Pedro can last a lifetime. Regular dental check-ups and good oral hygiene are essential to ensure their longevity.

### 2\. Are dental implants painful? 

The procedure itself is not painful, as it is performed under local anesthesia. Post-procedure discomfort is usually mild and can be managed with over-the-counter pain medications.

### 3\. What is the success rate of dental implants? 

Dental implants have a high success rate, typically above 95%. Factors such as the expertise of the dental surgeon and the patient’s overall health can influence the outcome.

### 4\. How do I know if I am a candidate for dental implants?

 Most individuals with good general and oral health and sufficient jawbone density are suitable candidates. A consultation with the dental professional at Dr. Smile can confirm your eligibility. If not, we can also suggest an alternative that suits your case the best. 

### 5\. What are the benefits of dental implants over other tooth replacement options?

Dental implants are durable, look and feel like natural teeth, prevent bone loss, and do not require altering adjacent teeth. They are a long-term solution that enhances both oral health and aesthetics.

### 6\. Are dental implants restorative, or are they related to cosmetic care? 

Dental implants are like two-edged swords. They are both restorative and are associated with cosmetic care. Where on one end it replaces missing teeth and restores function, simultaneously it also enhances aesthetics, making them integral to both oral health care and cosmetic dentistry.
