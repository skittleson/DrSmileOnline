## Frequently  Asked Questions

### Q. How often should I schedule routine checkups?

Most people benefit from visiting every six months. Regular checkups help catch small issues before they become bigger problems.

### Q. What can I do to reduce anxiety before an appointment?

Try deep breathing, listening to calming music, or bringing a trusted friend along. Communicating your concerns with the provider also helps ease nerves.

### Q. Are X-rays safe for children and adults?

Yes. Modern digital X-rays use very low radiation levels, making them safe for all ages while providing clear diagnostic images.

### Q. What’s the difference between preventive and restorative care?

Preventive care focuses on avoiding problems through cleanings and education, while restorative care repairs damage such as cavities or broken teeth.

### Q. How do I know if a provider is the right fit for my family?

Look for someone who listens, explains clearly, and creates a welcoming environment. Reviews and recommendations from others can also guide your choice.
