## What Is a Gummy Smile?

### An overview 

A captivating smile, as they say, is the hallmark of confidence, but for people with a gummy smile, it’s only self-consciousness that takes center stage. 

-   A gummy smile, or excessive gingival display, occurs when too much gum tissue is visible above the upper teeth when smiling. 
-   Anyone who deviates from the golden proportion has their smile considered “gummy” if more than 3–4 millimeters of gum tissue shows. 
-   Though it’s not a medical concern, it can affect self-esteem and how freely someone smiles.

### Causes a Gummy Smile

Understanding the root cause is key to choosing the right treatment. Common causes include

-   This can be in genetics
-   Excess gum tissue covering the teeth
-   Short or worn-down teeth
-   A hyperactive upper lip that lifts too high when smiling
-   Abnormal jaw development or vertical maxillary excess
-   Genetics or medication-induced gum overgrowth

Each case is unique, which is why a personalized consultation with a Cosmetic Dentist in Newport Beach is essential.

### 3\. Lip Repositioning Surgery

This is for patients with a hyperactive upper lip; this procedure lowers the lip’s resting position to reduce gum exposure when smiling. Let’s see how this process functions.

-   It involves making incisions in the mouth.
