## Frequently asked questions 

### Q. What is Gum Treatment and how does it work?

Gum disease treatment typically starts with a deep cleaning procedure called scaling and root planing, which removes plaque and tartar from below the gumline. Depending on severity, treatments may include antibiotics, laser therapy, or surgical options like flap surgery or bone grafts.

### Q.. Is Gum Disease Treatment painful?

Most treatments are performed under local anesthesia, so discomfort is minimal during the procedure. Post-treatment soreness is common but manageable with over-the-counter pain relief and proper aftercare.

### Q. How long does it take to show results?

You may notice improvements in gum health within a few days to weeks, especially reduced bleeding and swelling. Full healing from surgical treatments can take several weeks, with follow-up visits ensuring progress.

### Q. Can Gum Disease Treatment reverse receding gums?

While treatment can stop further recession and improve gum health, reversing recession often requires soft tissue grafts or regenerative procedures. Your dentist will recommend the best approach based on your condition.

### Q. Who should consider getting this treatment?

Anyone experiencing symptoms like bleeding gums, bad breath, gum recession, or loose teeth should consider treatment. Early intervention can prevent tooth loss and other serious complications.
