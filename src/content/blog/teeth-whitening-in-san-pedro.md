## Professional whitening vs. store-bought kits

### Customized to your teeth

Every smile stains differently. A store kit is one-size-fits-all; a professional session starts with a real evaluation of your teeth to identify the type and depth of staining before any whitening gel goes on.

### Faster, more noticeable results

Drugstore kits typically take weeks of daily application to show a modest change. Professional teeth whitening in San Pedro can show a major difference in a single visit.

### Less sensitivity

At-home kits are notorious for leaving teeth feeling "zapped." Professional-grade gels are formulated with soothing ingredients so most patients stay comfortable through the process.

### Better gum protection

Generic drugstore trays don't fit precisely, so the bleaching gel can leak onto the gums and cause irritation or burns. In-office whitening uses a custom-fitted barrier that keeps the gel where it belongs — on your teeth.

## What a whitening session looks like

1.  **The checkup.** A quick look at your teeth to confirm there's no decay that needs attention first.
2.  **The plan.** A conversation about how dramatic a change you want, and a plan that fits your budget and schedule.
3.  **The prep.** Your gums are protected with a barrier — the step home kits skip entirely.
4.  **The whitening.** The professional-grade gel goes to work while you relax.
5.  **The reveal.** You leave the same visit with a noticeably brighter smile.

## What whitening can't fix

Whitening lifts surface stains, but it doesn't change deep internal discoloration, chips, or gaps. If that's what you're dealing with, [**dental veneers**](/DrSmileOnline/dental-veneers-guide/) are usually the better fit — many patients start with whitening and decide from there whether veneers make sense for a more uniform result.

## Keeping your results bright

-   Drink coffee, tea, and red wine through a straw, or rinse with water afterward.
-   Keep up regular brushing to stop new surface stains before they set.
-   Ask about custom take-home trays for occasional touch-ups.
-   Come in for regular cleanings — they clear surface stains before whitening is even needed.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q. Is professional teeth whitening safe for my enamel?

Yes. Done by a professional, whitening is formulated to brighten teeth without wearing down enamel — that's part of why the evaluation happens before any gel is applied.

### Q. Will my teeth feel sensitive afterward?

Some patients notice mild sensitivity that fades quickly. The gels used in-office include ingredients specifically meant to keep you comfortable during and after the session.

### Q. Can I whiten veneers or crowns?

No — whitening only works on natural enamel. If you have veneers or crowns, they won't change shade with whitening gel; ask about your options if you want everything to match.

### Q. How long do whitening results last?

It varies by person, but most patients enjoy their results for several months to a year with good habits — cutting back on staining drinks, brushing regularly, and coming in for touch-ups as needed.
