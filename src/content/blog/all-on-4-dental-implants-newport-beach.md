## What Are All on 4 Dental Implants?

The all on 4 dental implants technique is a groundbreaking method for full-mouth teeth restoration. It involves placing just four dental implants in each jaw to support a fixed dental prosthesis. These implants are strategically positioned to provide maximum stability without the need for bone grafting, a common requirement in traditional implant procedures.

Unlike conventional methods that may require up to seven implants, the All-on-4 procedure minimizes the number of implants while maximizing efficiency and comfort. This approach ensures immediate functionality, allowing patients to leave the dental office with a new set of teeth in a single day.

## How the Procedure Works

The All-on-4 process is streamlined for patient convenience and optimal results. Here’s a step-by-step overview:

1.  **Consultation and Evaluation**: During the initial visit, Dr. Javid assesses the patient’s oral health, including the condition of the jawbone, gums, and any remaining teeth.
2.  **Treatment Planning**: Advanced imaging technology is used to map out the precise placement of the implants, ensuring stability and longevity.
3.  **Implant Placement**: Four implants are surgically placed in the upper or lower jaw. Two implants are positioned vertically in the front, while the remaining two are angled in the back to maximize bone support.
4.  **Prosthesis Attachment**: A temporary fixed bridge is attached to the implants on the same day, providing immediate functionality and aesthetics.
5.  **Healing and Integration**: Over the next 3-6 months, the implants fuse with the jawbone, creating a strong foundation for the permanent prosthesis.
6.  **Final Prosthesis Placement**: Once the implants are fully integrated, the temporary bridge is replaced with a custom-designed permanent prosthesis.

## Benefits of All on 4 Dental Implants

Opting for all on 4 dental implants offers numerous advantages over traditional dentures and other implant techniques:

1.  **Immediate Results:** One of the most significant benefits of the All-on-4 procedure is its immediate functionality. Patients can leave the dental office with a fully restored smile on the same day as their surgery.

2.  **Minimally Invasive:** By using only four implants and avoiding bone grafting, the procedure is less invasive than traditional methods. This reduces surgical time, recovery periods, and associated discomfort.

3.  **Long-Lasting Solution:** With proper care, All-on-4 implants can last for decades. The implants are designed to fuse with the jawbone, providing a stable and permanent foundation for the prosthesis.

4.  **Natural Appearance:** The fixed dental prosthesis is custom-made to match the natural look of teeth. This ensures a beautiful, lifelike smile that enhances the patient’s confidence.

5.  **Improved Functionality:** Unlike dentures, All-on-4 implants do not shift or require adhesives. They function like natural teeth, allowing patients to eat, speak, and smile comfortably.

6.  **Cost-Effective:** Since fewer implants are required and bone grafting is unnecessary, the All-on-4 procedure is often more cost-effective than traditional full-mouth restoration techniques.
