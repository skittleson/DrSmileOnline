## Key services offered by General Dentists

This section will highlight the key services of a Local General Dentist in Lomita

General dentists are like the “family doctors” of dentistry. They provide a wide range of services to keep your teeth healthy. Here are some of the most important ones:

-   Preventive Care: Regular cleanings, fluoride treatments, and dental education to stop problems before they start.
-   Checkups and Exams: Dentists check for cavities, gum disease, and other issues during routine visits.
-   Fillings and Restorations: If you have a cavity, the dentist can remove the decay and fill the tooth.
-   Crowns and Bridges: These protect damaged teeth or replace missing ones.
-   Root Canals: A treatment to save teeth that are badly infected.
-   Dentures and Implants: Options for replacing missing teeth.
-   Cosmetic Dentistry: Services like teeth whitening, veneers, and bonding to improve the look of your smile.
-   Orthodontics: Straightening teeth with braces or clear aligners like Invisalign.
-   Emergency Care: Help for sudden problems like toothaches, broken teeth, or infections.
