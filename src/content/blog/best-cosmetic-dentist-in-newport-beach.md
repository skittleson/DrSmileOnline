## Frequently asked questions:

### **Why is dental hygiene essential?** 

Maintaining dental hygiene prevents cavities, gum disease, and bad breath. Regular brushing, flossing, and professional cleanings protect your oral health and enhance your smile.

### **What should I look for in a dentist?** 

Consider qualifications, experience, reviews, office location, and patient comfort. A trustworthy dentist prioritizes your well-being with transparent communication, modern technology, and comprehensive services.

### **How often should I visit the dentist?** 

Visit the dentist every six months for routine checkups. Additional visits may be needed for specific issues like cavities, gum disease, or orthodontic treatments.

### **What is included in routine dental services?** 

Routine services include dental exams, cleaning, X-rays, cavity fillings, and gum disease prevention. These services aim to maintain oral health and catch problems early.

### **Are emergency dental services available?** 

Yes, we offer emergency services for urgent issues like severe pain, trauma, or infections. Contact your dentist immediately in emergencies for prompt care.
