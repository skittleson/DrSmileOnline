You bite into something hard, or catch an elbow at practice, and suddenly a tooth is chipped or cracked. The first question is always the same: what do I do right now? **Broken tooth repair in Torrance** is available the same day, but the next few minutes still matter for how well it goes.

## What to do in the first few minutes

-   Rinse your mouth gently with warm water.
-   If it's bleeding, apply gentle pressure with clean gauze.
-   Use a cold compress on the outside of your cheek to bring down swelling.
-   Save any broken piece of the tooth — wrap it in a damp cloth or bring it in a small container.
-   Avoid chewing on that side of your mouth until you're seen.
-   If a sharp edge is cutting your tongue or cheek, cover it temporarily with dental wax or sugarless gum.

None of this fixes the tooth — it just protects it and manages pain until you're in the chair.

## What kind of break are you dealing with?

Treatment depends on where the break is and how deep it runs:

-   **Chipped or cracked enamel** — the outer layer is damaged, the pulp is untouched; usually bonding or a crown.
-   **Fractured cusp** — a chewing point breaks off; often a crown or onlay, sometimes in one visit.
-   **Cracked tooth** — a crack running toward the root; may need a root canal and crown, or extraction if it extends below the gumline.
-   **Split tooth** — the tooth has separated into two pieces; part may be savable, or the tooth may need extraction and an implant.

A clinical exam and digital X-ray is the only reliable way to tell which one you have — that's the first thing that happens when you arrive.

## Can it be fixed the same day?

For most fracture types, yes. Chips and fractured cusps are commonly bonded or crowned the same visit at Dr. Smile's Torrance office. Even cases needing a root canal are frequently completed in a single appointment, using digital imaging to assess the damage as soon as you arrive.
