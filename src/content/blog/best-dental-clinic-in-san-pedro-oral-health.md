## Frequently Asked Questions :

### Q. Why should I visit a dental clinic regularly?

Regular visits help catch problems early, like cavities or gum disease, before they get worse. Dentists also clean areas you can’t reach with brushing and flossing. This keeps your teeth healthier, saves money on bigger treatments, and helps you maintain a confident smile.

### Q. Are dental cleanings really necessary if I brush daily?

Yes. Brushing and flossing remove most plaque, but not all. Over time, tartar builds up and can only be removed by professional cleaning. Regular cleanings prevent gum disease, cavities, and bad breath, making them an important part of keeping your mouth healthy.

### Q. Do dental treatments hurt?

Modern dentistry uses advanced tools and techniques to make treatments comfortable. Local anesthesia or sedation is often used to reduce pain. Most patients feel little to no discomfort during procedures, and dentists focus on making the experience as stress-free as possible.

### Q. How do I know if I need braces or aligners?

If your teeth are crowded, crooked, or your bite feels uneven, braces or aligners may help. Dentists or orthodontists examine your teeth and jaw, then recommend the best option. Straightening teeth improves both appearance and oral health by making cleaning easier.

### Q. Can dental health affect overall health?

Yes. Poor oral health is linked to heart disease, diabetes, and other conditions. Infections in the mouth can spread to the body. Keeping teeth and gums healthy not only protects your smile but also supports your overall well-being and long-term health.
