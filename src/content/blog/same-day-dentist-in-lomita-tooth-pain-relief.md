## When You Need an Emergency Dentist in Lomita: Specific Situations

Here are the situations when you’ll definitely need an emergency dentist –

### 1\. Cracked or Broken Tooth

A cracked tooth can feel sharp, dull, or somewhere in between. The treatment depends on how deep the crack goes:

-   Minor chips are often repaired with dental bonding — quick and painless
-   Moderate cracks may need a dental crown to protect the remaining structure
-   Deep fractures near the root can require a root canal before placing a crown

### 2\. Sudden Severe Pain

Pain that comes on strong and fast — especially at night — often points to an infection or pulp damage inside the tooth. This is not something to manage with painkillers alone. An **[emergency dentist in Lomita](https://doctorsmileonline.com/bleeding-gums-emergency-dentist-in-lomita/)** can identify the source and provide real treatment, not just temporary relief.

### 3\. Wisdom Tooth Pain

This one catches people off guard. Wisdom tooth removal in Lomita is one of the most common reasons patients seek urgent care. When a wisdom tooth is impacted or partially erupted, it creates intense pressure and pain that can radiate into the jaw and ear.

## What to Do Before You Arrive

While you are on your way to Dr. Smile, these steps can help manage the situation:

-   Knocked-out tooth: Keep it moist — place it in milk or hold it gently between your cheek and gum
-   Cracked tooth: Rinse with warm water, avoid chewing on that side
-   Swelling: Apply a cold pack to the outside of your cheek in short intervals
-   Bleeding: Apply gentle pressure with clean gauze or cloth
-   Pain: Take an over-the-counter pain reliever as directed — do not place aspirin directly on the gum

These steps will not fix the issue. Only a same-day dentist can do that. But they can make the wait more manageable.
